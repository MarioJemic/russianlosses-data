# Data Sources and Reuse

No blanket license is granted for this collection. Upstream publishers retain
their rights and terms. This mirror does not relicense third-party data as MIT,
CC0 or public domain. Retain source attribution, dates and methodology when
reusing observations, and review source-specific restrictions before redistribution
or commercial reuse.

Source attribution is retained in `data/v1/manifest.json` and individual JSON
envelopes. CSV users should retain the corresponding JSON metadata.

Main publishers include:

- [Russian Casualties in Ukraine API](https://russian-casualties.in.ua/): reported loss series.
- [Conflict Investigations / NZZ Maps](https://github.com/conflict-investigations/nzz-maps): national territory areas.
- [DeepStateMap](https://deepstatemap.live/): dated territorial-control geometry.
- [Ukraine COD administrative boundaries](https://data.humdata.org/dataset/cod-ab-ukr): administrative geometry, distributed through geoBoundaries; source metadata identifies CC BY 3.0 IGO.
- [Transparency International](https://www.transparency.org/en/cpi): corruption perceptions.
- [World Bank dataset terms](https://www.worldbank.org/en/about/legal/terms-of-use-for-datasets): WDI, Gini and WGI, including any third-party exceptions.
- [Reporters Without Borders](https://rsf.org/en/index): World Press Freedom Index editions.
- [National Bank of Ukraine](https://bank.gov.ua/en/open-data/api-dev): exchange rates.
- [GWIS](https://gwis.jrc.ec.europa.eu/) and [Copernicus EFFIS](https://effis.jrc.ec.europa.eu/): fire estimates and historical products; consult each export's terms.
- [NASA FIRMS](https://firms.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/active_fire/) and [NASA data policy](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/engage/open-data-services-software/data-use-policy): thermal detections. Derived control labels also retain DeepState attribution.
- Budget observations retain their individual publication links in the dataset.

The collection contains observations and derived estimates, not copied news
articles. No completeness, accuracy, fitness-for-purpose or uninterrupted-update
guarantee is made. Consult the original sources for revisions and authoritative
definitions. Report suspected data errors through this repository's issues.
